Here is exactly what you will find inside the latest release:

📦 1. Extensive & Updatable Prompt Library (34 for Text + 26 for Image)

The app comes fully loaded with 34 Text Prompts and 26 Image Prompts, covering almost every aspect of your daily life, work, and creativity:

· Text Prompts (34): Includes templates for Education (summaries, study plans, quizzes), Work (CVs, cover letters, emails, business plans), Personal & Hobbies (poems, Kabary, love letters), and Health/Self-Development.
· Image Prompts (26): Highly detailed templates for Photorealistic Portraits, Surreal Art, 3D Glassmorphism, Cinematic Cityscapes, and Action Sports posters.
· 🔄 Updatable Content: The library is not static! The app features a built-in updater that allows you to download new prompt packs (ZIP files) directly from GitHub. You will never run out of fresh ideas.

⚙️ 2. Smart "Fill-in-the-Blanks" Customization

Every prompt is interactive. Instead of just copying static text, the app generates dynamic input fields for variables. You simply fill in the blanks with your own ideas, guided by helpful examples (e.g., Oh: Cyberpunk, Ex: Paris, e.g. 8k resolution), and the app perfectly structures your custom prompt.

🌍 3. Full Multilingual Support

The entire app, including the interfaces, categories, and prompt examples, is fully translated into three languages:

· 🇲🇬 Malagasy
· 🇫🇷 French
· 🇬🇧 English

📴 4. Offline & Online Capabilities

· Offline Mode: Browse, read, and customize all 69+ prompts without an internet connection.
· Online Mode: Check for updates and seamlessly import new prompt ZIP files to keep your app refreshed with the latest AI trends.

✨ 5. Premium UI/UX Design

Inside, the app features a beautiful 3D Glassmorphism interface. It includes smooth animations (like sliding splash screens), a sleek dark theme, intuitive tab layouts (Text/Image), and a modern language selector, making the user experience feel highly professional, fast, and premium.
