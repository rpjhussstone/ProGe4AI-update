This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application **ProGe4AI** (the "App") handles your information. We have designed our App with a strict privacy-first, offline-first approach.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
**We do not collect, store, or share any personal data.** 
The App operates entirely offline to generate text-based prompts. We do not have servers that track your usage, and we do not require you to create an account or log in.

### 2. No Access to Photos or Camera
The App is a prompt generation tool. **It does not request, access, or interact with your device's Camera or Photo Gallery.** No images are processed or stored by the App.

### 3. Internet Usage and Downloads
The App only requires an internet connection for one specific and optional reason:
*   **Downloading Prompt Packs:** To download new text prompt packs (in ZIP or JSON format) hosted on our public GitHub repository. 
During this process, no personal data from your device is transmitted. 

### 4. How the App Works
The App allows you to customize text prompts by filling in variables. You can then copy these generated prompts and use them in third-party AI image generators (such as ChatGPT, Midjourney, etc.). Because the App only generates text, any images created using these prompts are handled entirely outside of ProGe4AI, and we assume no responsibility for how third-party tools process them.

### 5. Device Permissions
The App only requires:
*   **Internet Access:** Required solely to download the prompt packs mentioned in Section 3.
You can revoke this permission at any time in your Android device settings, though it will prevent pack downloads.

### 6. Third-Party Services
